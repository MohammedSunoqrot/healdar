HealdarFlags.woff2

A subset of the "Twemoji Country Flags" font (country-flag-emoji-polyfill),
cut down to the regional-indicator letters Healdar's flags use (A, E, Q, S, U:
Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, EU, USA). Windows has no flag emoji and shows the
two letters instead; this font draws the flags.
